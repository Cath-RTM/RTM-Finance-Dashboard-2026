Formula: EBITDA = (Sales − Operational Expense) + Interest + Taxes (Payroll) + Depreciation + Amortization Sources: QuickBooks P&L (authoritative). Interest = QB "Interest Paid" (RTM) / "Interest Expense" (U360). D&A = $0 (not recorded in QB for either entity). Dec figures (FY2022–2025) include QB year-end payroll tax true-up adjustments.
RTM FY2023 was peak performance
$6.16M revenue and $2.52M EBITDA at 40.9% margin — the strongest year on record. The business demonstrated it can sustain 40%+ margins at scale.
FY2024–25 margin compression
RTM margins dropped to 30.5% (FY24) and 29.0% (FY25) due to cost growth outpacing revenue. FY2026 YTD is recovering strongly back to 38.9%.
U360 is recovering
U360 EBITDA improved from -$936K (FY23) → -$776K (FY24) → -$216K (FY25) → near breakeven in FY26 YTD. The loss trajectory is clearly reversing.
Combined story is positive
Combined EBITDA of $739K in just Jan–May 2026 at 36.6% margin — annualizes to ~$1.77M, recovering toward the FY22–23 levels.
Dec true-ups distort monthly view
December figures each year include large payroll tax year-end adjustments. Use annual totals for trend analysis, not individual months.
D&A = $0 across all years
Neither RTM nor U360 records Depreciation or Amortization in QuickBooks. If a separate depreciation schedule exists, those figures can be added as ITDA components.
